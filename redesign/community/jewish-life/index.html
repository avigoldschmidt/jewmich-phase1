Kosher in Ann Arbor

Ann Arbor has limited kosher restaurants, but several markets carry kosher products. Always check certifications before you buy.

Local markets (verify hours and stock):

  • Busch’s Fresh Food Market — 2240 S Main St
  • Kroger — 3615 Washtenaw
  • Trader Joe’s — 2398 East Stadium Blvd
  • Costco — 771 Airport Blvd

U-M cafeteria: South Quad has offered a Glatt Kosher station (Seal-K) during the school year — confirm current status with dining or the House.

Metro Detroit has fuller kosher markets (including The Grove in Southfield). The House can sometimes help arrange delivery for a small fee — contact us.
