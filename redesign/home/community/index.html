Who we are

Since 1975, Chabad House of Ann Arbor has been a Jewish community center in the truest sense — the nerve center of Jewish educational and outreach activities here, serving students, local residents, and visitors together at 715 Hill Street.

Congregation life includes Shabbat and holiday services, communal meals, a listening ear, study, Hebrew School, the mikvah, and adult learning — alongside the student programs that share the same House. Programming follows the Chabad-Lubavitch movement, with over 6,000 centers worldwide; each local center is supported by the community it serves.
