What is Chabad?

Chabad provides a family-style environment for Jewish students at the University of Michigan. Students of every background and every level of observance are welcome to stop by, relax, schmooze, and connect.

Led by the Chabad Rabbi and Rebbetzin, students can join classes, community service, Israel programs, personal guidance, a daily minyan shared with Hillel, and traditional Shabbat and holiday experiences throughout the school year. Since 1975 at 715 Hill Street.
