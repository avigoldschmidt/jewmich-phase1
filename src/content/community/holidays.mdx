Celebrate the Jewish year with Congregation Chabad at 715 Hill Street. Times and menus change by season — use the registration links below, or [contact us](/contact) for confirmed details. We do not invent unverified schedules.

## High Holidays

Warm, meaningful, easy-to-follow Rosh Hashanah and Yom Kippur services. **No fees. No tickets. Everyone is welcome.**

Community members register through the community High Holiday form. University of Michigan students should use the [student holidays](/students/holidays) registration. You may also call [734-995-3276](tel:+17349953276).

Sponsors help make the High Holidays possible — [donate](/donate) if you would like to help.

## Chometz

Before Passover, sell your chometz online. Prefer the Zeffy path when available; a ChabadMS card option is also linked when offered for the season.

## Sukkot — lulav & etrog

Order a lulav and etrog set when the seasonal form is open.

## Passover

Join a warm, welcoming Seder with homemade food and the full Passover experience. Community Seder registration opens when the season begins; students use the [student Passover](/students/holidays#passover) path.

You can also reserve by calling [734-995-3276](tel:+17349953276). Questions about dress, length, or food are answered on the student holidays Passover section — the same welcoming experience applies.
