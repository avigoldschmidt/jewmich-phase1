Practical Jewish living resources for Ann Arbor — students and community together.

## Mikvah Israel

The community mikvah (ritual bath) is at Chabad House, 715 Hill Street (corner of Oakland). It is an ultramodern facility renovated with help from Mikvah USA, with a filtering pool heating system — the only such facility between Detroit and Chicago.

Mikvah Israel supports and maintains the mikvah. A nominal donation is asked of occasional users. Women of Mikvah Israel act as *shomerot* (assistants) for those who desire help. Educational material and speakers are available for anyone interested in learning more about this mitzvah.

Use the inquiry form below, or [contact us](/contact). Support the mikvah through [Donate](/donate) when campaigns are open.

## Mezuzah

A mezuzah is a parchment scroll written by a professional scribe, containing portions of the Torah that discuss Jewish faith — including the Shema. It is traditionally attached to the doorposts of a Jewish home. Maimonides writes that the mezuzah reminds us of G-d’s presence and unity.

Inexpensive kosher mezuzot (great for dorms and campus life) can be purchased at the Chabad House, 715 Hill Street. The House sells mezuzot **at cost as a service to the community** (historically around $72 for a scroll with a simple clear case — confirm current price when you order). You may use your own case. Request a purchase or mezuzah check through the form below, or ask us to point you to a broader selection.

## Kosher in Ann Arbor

Ann Arbor has a vibrant Jewish community but **limited kosher restaurants**. Several local markets carry kosher meats and other products — always check reliable kosher symbols before you buy.

**Supermarkets in Ann Arbor (verify hours and stock):**

- **Busch’s Fresh Food Market** — 2240 S Main St, Ann Arbor, MI 48103 · 734-998-2666
- **Kroger** — 3615 Washtenaw, Ann Arbor, MI 48104 · 734-677-2370
- **Trader Joe’s** — 2398 East Stadium Blvd, Ann Arbor, MI 48104 · 734-975-2455
- **Costco Warehouse** — 771 Airport Blvd, Ann Arbor, MI 48108 · 734-213-8010

**U-M cafeteria:** As of September 2018, the University opened a Glatt Kosher station (Seal-K) with lunch and dinner during the school year at **South Quad**, 600 East Madison. Open to U-M students and the public September–April; closed on Shabbat and holidays. Confirm current status with dining or the House.

**Metro Detroit (about 45 minutes):** fuller kosher markets and restaurants operate under Detroit Council of Orthodox Rabbis standards. Examples historically listed by the House include The Grove Kosher Market and Superior Meats (Southfield), Zeman’s New York Kosher Bakery (Oak Park), Bake Station, Jerusalem Pizza, Pizza Stop, Prime 10, Soul Cafe, and Wok In Cari Out. Delivery from some Detroit-area places can sometimes be arranged with House help for a small fee — [contact us](/contact).
