Learning for adults at every level — no background required. There is no charge for Study Center classes; they are open to the public. Private classes are available upon request.

## Jewish Learning Institute (JLI)

Chabad House of Ann Arbor hosts the Rohr Jewish Learning Institute. The curriculum is designed to challenge your thinking and warm your heart — whether you have taken classes before or are coming for the first time. Ask which course is running now, and register when the current series is open.

## Study Center — T.E.A.C.H.

**Torah Education At Chabad House.** A full range of exciting classes in Judaism. Schedules are subject to change — [contact us](/contact) to confirm.

Typical offerings (confirm current times):

- Tanya / Jewish mysticism
- Weekly Torah portion (including a women’s group)
- Jewish law
- Jewish philosophy
- Parsha ’n Pasta
- Challah baking
- Talmud study
- Shabbat-afternoon mystical insights and Jewish ethics

## Jewish Women’s Circle

A multi-denominational group of Jewish women who meet for fun Jewish events. Founded by Esther Goldstein as a way to unite Jewish women of all backgrounds.

Past and typical gatherings have included challah baking, Soup in the Sukkah, author evenings, Tu B’Shvat cooking, Havdalah café, salad workshops, Tea and Torah, book study, community volunteering, and an annual women’s trip to New York. Event dates and fees change by season — use the signup form and ask for the current schedule.

Helpful links often shared with the Circle: recipes, women and spirituality resources, [candle lighting](/candle-lighting), and the Circle’s Facebook presence for Washtenaw County.
