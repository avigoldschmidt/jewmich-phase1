Join Congregation Chabad for prayer, kiddush, and community at Chabad House of Ann Arbor, 715 Hill Street.

Congregation life includes Shabbat and holiday services, communal meals, learning, and a listening ear for local residents and visitors — alongside the student programs that share the same House.

### Service times

Please [contact us](/contact) for the current daily and holiday schedule. We publish times on this page only after staff confirm them — schedules change, and we will not invent them.

**Candle lighting** for Ann Arbor is available via [Chabad.org](/candle-lighting). During summer months the House often brings Shabbat in early; confirm the week’s practice when you call.

### Kiddush luncheon

After Shabbat morning prayers — typically just after noon — the congregation gathers to say the blessing over wine (Kiddush). Sponsoring a Kiddush is a traditional way to mark a birthday, anniversary, yahrzeit, baby naming, graduation, or other simcha with family and friends.

A typical Kiddush at Chabad includes about **25–45 people**, plus guests you invite. Luncheons for ~45 people historically cost about **$360–$500**, depending on how elaborate you make the meal. Fresh flowers for the buffet are always welcome. The Rabbi or Rebbetzin can help the sponsor prepare a few words about the occasion or the weekly Torah portion.

Bring food Thursday evening or Friday (uncut, in containers, with reliable pareve hechshers — OU, OK, and others the House accepts). The House kitchen handles washing and cutting for Shabbat. Full menu ideas and shopping notes are available when you [sponsor a Kiddush](/donate#sponsor-shabbat) or call [734-995-3276](tel:+17349953276).
