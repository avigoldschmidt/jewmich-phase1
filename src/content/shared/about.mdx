Welcome to the Chabad House of Ann Arbor at the University of Michigan.

## Our mission

The Chabad House of Ann Arbor is a Jewish community center in the truest sense — the nerve center of Jewish educational and outreach activities in Ann Arbor, serving the needs of the entire Jewish community: students, local residents, and visitors.

## History

The Rebbe, Rabbi Menachem Mendel Schneerson, of righteous memory, constantly called for the expansion of activities in existing Chabad Houses and the establishment of new Chabad Houses wherever Jews live — in cities, suburbs, and on college campuses throughout the nation and around the world.

In **1975** the Chabad House opened its doors for the first time, and has been serving the community ever since — more than 50 years of Jewish life at 715 Hill Street.

## Dual purpose

The Chabad House serves Ann Arbor in two intertwined ways:

1. A **home away from home** for Jewish students at the University of Michigan — Shabbat and holiday services and meals, and student-oriented programs. See the [student home](/home/students).
2. A place for services, meals, a listening ear, a helping hand, study, and community activities for local residents of greater Ann Arbor. See the [community home](/home/community).

Programming follows the Chabad-Lubavitch movement and philosophy, with over 6,000 centers worldwide. Each local center is supported by the community it serves.

## What students find here

Chabad provides a family-style environment for Jewish students at Michigan. Students of every background and every level of observance are welcome to stop by, relax, schmooze, and connect. Led by the Chabad Rabbi and Rebbetzin, students can join classes, community service, Israel programs, personal guidance, a daily minyan shared with Hillel, and traditional Shabbat and holiday experiences throughout the school year.
