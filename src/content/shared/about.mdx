Welcome to the Chabad House of Ann Arbor.

We are a Jewish community center in the truest sense — the nerve center of Jewish educational and outreach activities in Ann Arbor, serving students, local residents, and visitors.

## History

In **1975** the Chabad House opened its doors for the first time, and has been serving the community ever since — more than 50 years of Jewish life at 715 Hill Street.

## What we do

Chabad House serves a dual purpose:

1. A **home away from home** for Jewish students at the University of Michigan — Shabbat and holiday services and meals, and student-oriented programs.
2. A place for services, meals, a listening ear, study, and community activities for local residents of greater Ann Arbor.

Programming follows the Chabad-Lubavitch movement and philosophy, with over 6,000 centers worldwide. Each local center is supported by the community it serves.
