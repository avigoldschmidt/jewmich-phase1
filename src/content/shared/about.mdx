Chabad House of Ann Arbor is a warm Jewish home for University of Michigan students and for families across the Ann Arbor community.

Rooted in the Chabad-Lubavitch tradition, we open our doors for Shabbat, holidays, learning, and everyday kindness — without judgment, and with room for every Jew.

### Two doors, one house

**JewMich.com** welcomes students. **ChabadAnnArbor.com** welcomes the congregation and community. Both share this House, this address, and this mission.
