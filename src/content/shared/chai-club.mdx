**Chai Club** is a way to support Chabad House with a recurring monthly gift. Steady support helps us plan Shabbat dinners, youth programs, and community holidays with confidence.

### Chai Club and membership

Chai Club is **monthly philanthropic support**. For congregation membership levels and dues-style participation, see the [Membership](/membership) page.

Staff are confirming how these programs are described publicly; copy here stays intentionally soft until that policy is finalized.
