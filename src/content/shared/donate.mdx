Thank you for supporting Chabad House of Ann Arbor. Your gift helps us keep serving Jewish students at the University of Michigan and the greater Ann Arbor community — regardless of background or level of observance.

Chabad House of Ann Arbor is **not centrally funded**. We rely on people like you so we can continue providing a Jewish environment on campus and in town. That work includes:

- Shabbat and holiday services and meals
- Torah and Judaism classes
- Hospital and prison visitation
- Holiday programs and activities
- A Jewish library
- Private counseling and pastoral care
- Student activities and campus outreach
- Chabad Hebrew School (CHS)
- Camp Gan Israel
- Jewish Women’s Circle / Rosh Chodesh gatherings
- Community-wide Seder
- Jewish art calendar and seasonal programs
- Birthright and Israel programs for students
- Ritual mikvah
- Advocacy for kosher options on campus and at the hospital

Chabad House of Ann Arbor is a **501(c)(3)** nonprofit. Charitable contributions are tax-deductible.

### Chai Club (monthly)

A crucial part of support comes from people who commit to a **monthly** gift. Small amounts add up — eighteen is the numeric value of *chai* (life), so many donors choose multiples of $18 ($18, $36, $54, $72, $108, $180, and so on). You can increase, decrease, or cancel when you need to.

Chai Club is **monthly giving**. It is separate from congregation membership. {/* staff: confirm membership vs Chai Club policy — do not claim “no membership charge” */}

Set up monthly giving through the card/bank form below (choose the Chai Club / monthly option when offered), or call the House.

### Sponsor a Shabbat or Kiddush

- **Student Shabbat dinner** — dedicate a Friday night meal for students in someone’s honor. Use the sponsor form below.
- **Community Kiddush luncheon** — after Shabbat morning prayers the congregation gathers for Kiddush. Sponsorship marks a birthday, anniversary, yahrzeit, baby naming, graduation, or other simcha. A typical Kiddush serves about 25–45 people; luncheons for ~45 historically run in the **$360–$500** range depending on how elaborate you make it. The House can help with menu, hechsher guidance, and prep — use the kiddush form or [contact us](/contact).

### Wish list (in-kind)

Beyond cash gifts, the House sometimes needs specific in-kind help. Categories from the live wish list (confirm current needs before purchasing):

- **Lawn / landscape** — walkway, plantings, and grounds around 715 Hill
- **Furniture** — chairs, tables, and bookcases for the sanctuary and library
- **Paper goods** — plates, cups, napkins for Shabbat and holiday meals
- **Security** — monitoring equipment or service
- **Printing** — color brochures and program packets

Please [contact us](/contact) before dropping off items. Outdated furniture or obsolete computers often cost more to handle than they help — newer, useful gifts are most welcome.

### Life & Legacy

Chabad House is a pillar of Washtenaw County Jewish life — prayer, friendship, education, life-cycle moments, and a home-away-from-home for Michigan students. A legacy gift helps sustain that work for the next generation. Use the Life & Legacy form below, or ask the House for a letter of intent.
