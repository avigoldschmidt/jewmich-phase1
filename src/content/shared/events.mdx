**Weekly rhythm**

- **Student Shabbat dinner** — every Friday of the semester at 715 Hill. Five courses, no charge. [Reserve](/students/shabbat).
- **Community Shabbat & services** — [contact us](/contact) for current times until staff publish a confirmed schedule on [Services](/community/services).
- **Candle lighting** — [Ann Arbor times on Chabad.org](/candle-lighting).

**Seasonal programs** (open when registration is live): High Holidays, Sukkot, Chanukah / Latke Fest, Purim, Passover Seders, Birthright interest, Hebrew School, Camp Gan Israel, JLI and Jewish Women’s Circle, culinary workshops, and more. Student paths live under [Holidays](/students/holidays) and [Learn](/students/learn); community paths under [Community holidays](/community/holidays) and [Adult learning](/community/learn).

Current ticketed events and seasonal Zeffy registrations appear below when open. Past seasonal embeds are updated by staff.
