**Life & Legacy** invites friends of Chabad House to include Jewish life in Ann Arbor in their long-term plans — endowments, bequests, and other legacy gifts.

If you would like a confidential conversation about legacy giving, [contact the House](/contact). We will connect you with the right person and materials.
