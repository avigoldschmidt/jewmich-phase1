Is what you think about Chabad myth or fact? You may be surprised.

**Myth:** You have to be “Orthodox” to participate.  
**Fact:** Chabad is inclusive and non-judgmental. Programs are open to all. The vast majority of people who participate are not “Orthodox.”

**Myth:** Chabad’s goal is to make me Orthodox.  
**Fact:** Chabad is dedicated to engaging every Jew — regardless of background — and providing resources to grow in knowledge and commitment at your own pace.

**Myth:** Chabad does not consider Reform, Conservative, or non-practicing Jews as “real” Jews.  
**Fact:** We prefer not to label other Jews. Labels tend to divide. Every Jew is welcome.

**Myth:** Chabad is financed by headquarters in New York.  
**Fact:** Each center is supported by the community it serves. Funds donated locally stay local.
