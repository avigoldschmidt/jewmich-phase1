From time to time the House offers classes and gatherings online — adult learning, seasonal programs, and community check-ins when in-person meeting is not possible.

Meeting links and passwords change. We do **not** publish standing Zoom credentials on this page. For the current virtual schedule:

- [Contact us](/contact) by phone or form
- Watch the [newsletter](/newsletter) for announcements
- Ask about JLI, Jewish Women’s Circle, and other courses that sometimes include a remote option — see [Adult learning](/community/learn)

In-person life at 715 Hill remains the heart of the House. [Plan a visit](/visit) when you can join us there.
