## Visiting & living FAQ

Chabad frequently receives questions about living Jewishly in Ann Arbor. Answers to the most common ones:

**Are there kosher restaurants in Ann Arbor?**  
Limited. See the full listing on [Kosher in Ann Arbor](/community/jewish-life#kosher).

**Can I buy kosher meat and other products locally?**  
Several markets carry kosher products — details on the [kosher page](/community/jewish-life#kosher).

**Where can I purchase a mezuzah?**  
Inexpensive kosher mezuzot (great for dorms) are available at the Chabad House, 715 Hill Street. See [Mezuzah](/community/jewish-life#mezuzah).

**I’m moving to Ann Arbor — realtor suggestion?**  
The House has historically referred Adam Eichner with Eichner Realty (cell 734-709-5136). Confirm current recommendations when you call us — referrals can change.

**What times are services?**  
[Contact us](/contact) for the current daily and holiday schedule. We do not invent times on this site.

**Candle lighting this week?**  
See [candle lighting](/candle-lighting). During summer months the House often brings Shabbat in early; confirm when you call.

**Is there an eruv?**  
Not at this time surrounding Chabad House and local neighborhoods.

Have a question we didn’t cover? [Contact us](/contact) — we’re happy to help.
