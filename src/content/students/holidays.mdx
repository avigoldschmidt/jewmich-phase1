Celebrate the Jewish year with students at 715 Hill Street. Service and meal times change by season — reserve through the forms below, or [contact us](/contact) for current details. We do not post unverified times here.

## High Holidays

Reserve your seat for student High Holiday services and meals.

## Sukkot

Dinner in the sukkah — reserve through the student form. Second-night dinners are listed when offered.

## Chanukah

Latke Fest, Mega Challah Bake, and other Chanukah gatherings — sign up when registration opens.

## Purim

Purim celebrations and the student Purim gift service when available.

## Passover

Student seder registration runs through Zeffy when open for the season.
