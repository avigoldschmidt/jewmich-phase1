Jewish learning at Chabad meets you where you are — curious beginners, lifelong learners, and everyone in between.

### Sinai Scholars

A semester fellowship that pairs text study with conversation, mentorship, and a cohort of peers. Ask the House about the current semester’s cohort and stipend details.

### Study & conversation

Drop-in study, one-on-one learning with the rabbi or rebbetzin, and seasonal classes appear on the events hub and student channels.

Interested? [Contact us](/contact) or stop by 715 Hill — someone will help you find the right fit.
