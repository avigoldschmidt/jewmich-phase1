Explore Jewish learning at your own pace — interactive classes, one-on-one study, and signature student programs. No background required.

## Sinai Scholars

A Jewish educational experience built around fun, lively conversation.

Sinai Scholars is **not** a series of lectures and **not** a repeat of Hebrew school. It is a series of interactive discussions — fun, stimulating, deep, and relevant. It is a time to question, not only to listen. It is not geared to tell you what to think; it gives you a chance to explore Judaism’s rich heritage at your own pace, with you in the driver’s seat.

Participants explore central elements of Judaism that are relevant and empowering for all Jews, irrespective of background, education, or level of commitment. By the end of the journey, you will have a richer appreciation of core elements of Jewish heritage and identity — and how they interface with modern life.

Apply when the current cohort is open (form linked below), or [contact us](/contact) for dates.

## Study Center — T.E.A.C.H.

**Torah Education At Chabad House.** Chabad offers a full range of classes in Judaism. Classes are open to everyone; no prior knowledge is necessary. There is no charge, and they are open to the public. Private individual classes are also available upon request.

**Note:** Class schedules are subject to change — [contact us](/contact) to confirm what is meeting this week.
