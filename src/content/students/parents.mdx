Parents are always part of the House. Join a Friday night, dedicate a dinner in someone’s honor, or send a little care from home.

- **Visit for Shabbat** — add family guests when your student RSVPs, or call the House.
- **Sponsor a Shabbat** — underwrite a Friday night for students.
- **Send soup** — a classic care package path for parents and friends.
- **Give** — [Donate](/donate) or join [Chai Club](/donate#chai-club) monthly support.
