Parents are always part of the House. Join a Friday night, dedicate a dinner in someone’s honor, or send a little care from home.

## Visit for Shabbat

Add family guests when your student RSVPs for [Shabbat dinner](/students/shabbat), or call [734-995-3276](tel:+17349953276). Special family Shabbats are announced when offered.

## Sponsor a Shabbat

Underwrite a Friday night for students — a meaningful way to celebrate a birthday, anniversary, yahrzeit, or simply to say thank you. Use the [sponsor a Shabbat](https://www.jewmich.com/sponsorshabbat/) form or see [Donate](/donate#sponsor-shabbat).

## Send soup

Are you or someone you know under the weather? Enjoy a delicious bowl of chicken soup — or be a SOUPer friend.

One of Judaism’s great mitzvot is to assist a sick person. This volunteer-based program can deliver homemade chicken soup (just like Mom’s) to a friend’s door with a note saying it’s from you. Use the send-soup action below when the program is running, or [contact us](/contact) to arrange it.
