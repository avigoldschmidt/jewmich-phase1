### For University of Michigan students

Good food, great people, and your Friday night home on campus.

**Five courses · No charge · Come as you are**

Shabbat dinner happens every Friday of the semester. Services and dinner times change week to week — the RSVP form on jewmich.com shows the current Friday. You can also call [734-995-3276](tel:+17349953276) or WhatsApp the House.

Parents and family are always welcome — add them in the Notes field when you RSVP, or ask about family Shabbats.

**Not a current student?** Please call or WhatsApp rather than using the student form.

### How RSVP works

If you have reserved before, you can often continue with your phone number on the live form. New guests fill out name, email, phone, home address, class year, and how many people are coming (list guest names in Notes). Weekly text reminders are available on the form when offered.

### Help cook

Volunteer in the kitchen when you can — openings appear on the help-cook calendar below.
