## Birthright Israel

If you’re Jewish and have never been on an eligible group trip to Israel, this can be your chance.

Birthright Israel with **Mayanot** is a ~10-day trip — adventure, connection, and community. Typical itinerary moments (when offered) include the Dead Sea, Masada, the Jordan River, Jerusalem, and Tel Aviv. What’s usually included: round-trip flight from NYC, hotels, tours, and two meals a day. Travelers typically cover getting to NYC and a refundable deposit (amount announced per trip).

**Pre-registration / interest:** fill out the Birthright interest form so we can keep you updated. After submitting, you are directed to the official Mayanot registration when selections open.

Trip departure windows and “TBA” dates change by year — use the form and [contact us](/contact) for the current season rather than relying on outdated landing pages.
