Chabad Hebrew School and youth programs give children a joyful, hands-on Jewish education — holidays, Hebrew, and friendships that last.

This hub replaces the old `/chs/` front door. For enrollment, calendars, and the latest class details, reach out to the House — staff will point you to the current application and schedule.

### Who it is for

Families in Ann Arbor and nearby communities looking for warm, values-rich Jewish learning outside a day-school setting.
