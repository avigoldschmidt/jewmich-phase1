## Chabad Hebrew School

Dear parents — welcome to Chabad House of Ann Arbor Hebrew School, an exciting and innovative educational program in Ann Arbor.

Our school has a well-earned reputation as a trendsetter in creative Jewish education for children in **Kindergarten through Grade 7**, and for making every Jewish child feel welcome, regardless of knowledge or affiliation. Chabad Hebrew School is where Judaism comes alive and children are encouraged to ask and explore.

Experienced staff, a hands-on curriculum, and holiday workshops make the year educational and enjoyable. The curriculum is designed to instill a basic yet thorough understanding of our traditions and values, a strong sense of Jewish pride, and a love of Jewish life. We believe this holistic approach is the most effective way to educate children and ensure an enduring connection with our heritage.

Education has been key to Jewish spirituality and a cornerstone of our survival for thousands of years. By educating the young, we help ensure Jewish continuity.

Apply with the Hebrew School application when enrollment is open, or [contact us](/contact) for more information.
