## Chabad Hebrew School

An educational program for children in Kindergarten through Grade 7. Every Jewish child is welcome, regardless of knowledge or affiliation. Hands-on curriculum, holiday workshops, and a focus on Jewish pride and love of Jewish life.

Apply with the Hebrew School application when enrollment is open.

## Camp Gan Israel

Summer camp for kids — details and registration live on the Camp Gan Israel website.
