Visit & living in Ann Arbor

Visiting Chabad House or moving to Ann Arbor — what to expect, how to find us, and how we can help.

Whether you are touring campus, relocating for a job, or settling in for four years, Chabad House is ready to welcome you.

Visiting

Stop by 715 Hill Street, join a Shabbat meal, or send a note before you arrive so we can watch for you.

Living here

New to town? Ask about neighborhood tips, kosher options, holiday hospitality, and student or community programming that fits your stage of life.

This page merges the former visiting & living FAQ into one place. Detailed move-in forms and seasonal early-move-in payments still live on their legacy URLs until those flows are rebuilt.
